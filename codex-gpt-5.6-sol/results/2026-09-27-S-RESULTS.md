# Codex S: command-output coverage pilot, completed 2026-09-27

**19.71% lower aggregate token-priced cost; all 6 trials correct; all 3 task pairs cost less with TokenSaver.** Raw cost was $1.7757336 versus $1.4257016 with TokenSaver, a $0.3500320 reduction.

This six-trial development pilot is not an independent confirmation. It remains below the prospectively recorded 30% target. Earlier cohorts, including R's 15.15% pilot, remain separate; the difference between pilots is not a controlled estimate of this change alone.

## Completed work and results

Official Codex 0.152.1, GPT-5.6 Sol, high effort, running serially on Ubuntu under WSL. Each arm completed the same public Rust maintenance task against the same frozen starting source and acceptance checks. The model, effort, task definitions, graders and non-native runtime binaries were unchanged from R. TokenSaver used the native context engine, proxy compression and command-output handling.

## Accounting and evidence

All 107 recorded provider requests reconcile to the trial totals. Pricing applies the frozen study rate card per request: $4/M ordinary input, $0.40/M cached input, $5/M explicit cache writes and $20/M output including reasoning; long-context rates are $8/$0.80/$10/$30 above 272,000 request-context tokens. These are API-equivalent estimates, not subscription invoices or a claim about today's retail prices. Reasoning is included in output once. Savings are calculated from total cost: 100 × (1 − TokenSaver / raw), not the mean of task percentages.

The initial aggregate summaries contained cost bounds where individual request tiers were unavailable. The final request-by-request reconciliation resolves those tiers and supplies the costs quoted above.

Treatment recorded 33 hook invocations and 11 rewrites, two complete native source responses and one partial response. Exact activity counts are exported separately; the older audit field named native_queries counts complete responses only. Activity does not by itself establish a feature's monetary contribution.

The candidate extended the existing command-output path for a narrowly defined environment-prefixed Cargo command. Qualification passed 215 native tests, strict Clippy, 15 accounting tests and eight real-client loopback command cases before live inference. Source and study-artifact hashes were verified again after completion. This is a research configuration, not a production-release claim.
